# Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Gamepad | Touch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Accelerate | W / Up | Right trigger | automatic (Settings: Auto-accelerate) |
| Brake / reverse | S / Down | Left trigger | BRAKE |
| Steer | A D / Left Right | Left stick | `<` `>` buttons, or tilt the phone (Settings: Tilt steering) |
| Drift (handbrake) | Space | - | DRIFT |
| Horn | E | A (bottom button) | HONK |
| Reset car to road | R | Y (top button) | RESET |
| Pause | Esc | Start | II (top right) |

Touch buttons are true multi-touch: steer and brake with different fingers at once.
Tilt: hold the phone in landscape; the neutral angle is whatever you hold when the race starts. If it steers the wrong way,
turn on "Invert tilt" in Settings. "Show touch controls on PC" lets you preview the touch layout with a mouse.
